## Business Problem and Project Purpose 
Nonprofit organizations need a centralized system for managing their volunteers and community service opportunities. Volunteers also need a clean, effective way to report their volunteering hours. A common theme for organizations that host volunteering opportunities is that you have to directly contact the organizer, whether it's through email or phone. As well, many organizations tend to keep track of volunteers' hours either through paper or simple spreadsheets.
